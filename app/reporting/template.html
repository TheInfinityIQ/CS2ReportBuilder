Definitions used in this report

Opening kill
The first enemy-on-enemy death event in a complete round, credited to this player. Simultaneous first death ticks are classified as unknown.
Alive counts
Start from a verified five-player roster per side, subtract recorded deaths in time order, and withhold rounds with ambiguous sequences. Teamkills and world deaths affect counts but are not opening kills.
Death timing
Server tick difference divided by each match’s tick rate. This is not a reaction-time measurement. Missing streams are unavailable, never zero.
Authority and exclusions
Source validity, coaching validity and integrity must pass. Event-derived kills and deaths are used; cumulative engine scoreboards are not used to resolve them. Incomplete/split games are not automatically stitched. Only complete, unambiguous rounds enter the figures.
