## Application and coaching review

Use [evaluation cases](PLAYER_KNOWLEDGE_EVALUATION.md) in separate fresh sessions.
Record the actual output and evidence for each judgment. Diagnose failures as:

- Missing knowledge: the relevant distinction is absent from the supplied file.
- Access/application failure: it is present, but overlooked or applied incorrectly.
- Missing case evidence: required intent, geometry or state is not supplied.
- Workflow restriction: the coach's tools or output constraints prevent useful
  investigation or explanation despite available knowledge.

These can coexist; an unanswered question does not automatically justify another
paragraph. The knowledge file supplies background; test expectations remain out
of the persona handoff. No fresh receiving-model evaluation was run in this pass.

For a coaching review, ask for the specific claim, its support, the useful cue or
action, a condition that changes the advice, and any consequential omission.
Do not reward criticism or agreement frequency. Check correctness against
independent source/game evidence and usefulness through Dawson's short-output
feedback. Coach/persona agreement based on the same knowledge is correlated
evidence, not independent validation or demonstrated player improvement.
