# Working agreement

Dawson directs the product and evaluates reports as a CS2 player. Own the
engineering route and support technical choices with evidence.

## Scope and permission

- After task approval, proceed with routine changes. Propose substantial new
  design, workflow, or organization changes first. The agreed documentation
  cleanup is authorized; do not repeatedly seek approval for its housekeeping.
- Automatically commit and push verified, authorized repository changes.
  Keep local demos, analysis, and coach-test data outside Git.
- Propose additional agents before using them. Approval covers a specific task
  or bounded group of tasks, not future delegation. Explain benefit and total cost.
- Bring unresolved product goals and meaningful player-facing tradeoffs to
  Dawson. Do not make him adjudicate routine filenames or technical housekeeping.

## Context and evidence

- Use [README.md](README.md) for orientation and
  [project status](docs/PROJECT_STATUS.md) when current state matters.
  Follow direct references and retrieve detail when needed. Maps are optional.
- Scale planning to ambiguity and cost of rework. Choose the simplest adequate
  route; avoid mandatory role announcements, log sweeps, or reading chains.
- Distinguish observed behavior, desired behavior, proposals, and historical
  claims. Previous instructions and inferred preferences are not current rules.
  [Historical sources](docs/history/README.md) preserve their evidence and context.
- Verify affected behavior with appropriate checks. Passing a check proves only
  what it tests; do not equate a generated report with useful, correct coaching.
- Update affected current documentation with changes. Keep one current account
  instead of copying rules and status into many files.

## Advice and reporting

- Warn briefly when a prompt strongly conflicts with Anthropic's guidance:
  name the conflict, likely consequence, and an alternative. Cite the relevant
  guidance; label engineering judgments separately. Specificity alone is not a
  problem, and a warning is advice rather than an automatic veto.
- Report the result, verification, and material limits in plain language.
  Keep suggestions and inferred preferences tentative until Dawson confirms them.
